    How pacing works

    When a provider says it's busy, the app waits as long as it asks, three times at most, instead of hammering it. Identical read-only requests (re-reading the same job or CV) reuse the earlier answer: 0 tokens.

    How the key is used

    Bring your own key from tavily.com ↗, a search API built for AI tools (its free plan includes a monthly allowance of credits). Thorough research then makes two searches per company, 2 credits, sending only the company's name. The key stays in your settings file: never in the log, the export or a request to anyone but Tavily. Testing it uses no credits.
